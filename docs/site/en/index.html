Mai Budget

Your household budget at a glance: fixed costs, subscriptions, savings and what is left, without bank access or an account.

Coming soon to Google Play

What the app does

  • Overview: income, planned items and money left, in total and per person
  • Items with interval, account, provider and due date; the amount field calculates like a spreadsheet
  • Standing-order planner and fair split between partners, savings forecast with compound interest
  • Tips with estimated savings, widgets for home screen and watch

Your data stays yours

  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your entries stay encrypted on your device.
  • Backup and export only when you want, as a file you keep yourself.

All details are in the privacy policy.
